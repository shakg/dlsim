# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want DLSim to be a respectful, welcoming project. Everyone may participate
regardless of experience, identity, background or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and code, not personal traits.
- Accept feedback and correct mistakes.
- Respect privacy and personal boundaries.
- Put the health of the project and community ahead of personal conflict.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation or discrimination
- Sexualized language, imagery or unwanted attention
- Insults, trolling or personal and political attacks
- Publishing private information without permission
- Deliberate disruption of project discussions or work
- Conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This code applies to the repository, issue tracker, pull requests and other
project spaces. It also applies when someone represents the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report violations privately to the maintainer at
[ishakgonulgb@gmail.com](mailto:ishakgonulgb@gmail.com). Reports will be
reviewed promptly and kept confidential where possible.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, issue warnings,
temporarily restrict participation or permanently ban participants. The
response will be proportional to the behavior and its impact.

Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern is itself a violation.
